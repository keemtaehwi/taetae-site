Korean-Chinese Food Near Seongsu and Seoul Forest: Jjajangmyeon and Tangsuyuk at Taetae

Taetae (태태 TAETAE), a Korean-Chinese restaurant in Oksu-dong, Seongdong-gu, Seoul, is a good choice for jjajangmyeon and tangsuyuk after Seoul Forest or Seongsu. Go from Seoul Forest to Wangsimni on the Suin-Bundang Line, then take the Gyeongui-Jungang Line to Oksu Station. For two people, order Black Vinegar Sweet and Sour Pork (흑초 찹쌀 탕수육, ₩33,000) plus jjajang.

Written by the Taetae team · Updated 2026-11-24 · Prices as of Nov 2026

How do I get from Seoul Forest to Taetae?

Ride the Suin-Bundang Line one stop to Wangsimni, then the Gyeongui-Jungang Line two stops (via Eungbong) to Oksu Station.

  • Seoul Forest Station (Suin-Bundang Line) to Wangsimni: one stop.
  • Wangsimni to Oksu (Gyeongui-Jungang Line): two stops, with Eungbong in between.
  • Leave Oksu Station by Exit 7 and walk 340m, about 5 minutes.

The restaurant is on the 2nd floor of a building across the street from Raemian Oksu Riverzen, marked by a red 太太 sign. The last stretch to the door is a gentle uphill.

What should two people order at Taetae?

Share Black Vinegar Sweet and Sour Pork (흑초 찹쌀 탕수육) and add a jjajang each, or choose the 2-person set at ₩55,000.

What are the hours for an after-walk meal?

Open every day; lunch 11:00-15:00 (last order 14:30), dinner 17:00-22:00 (last order 20:30).

  • Break time is 15:00-17:00, so a mid-afternoon walk in Seongsu may not fit.
  • Plan to arrive before the 20:30 dinner last order.

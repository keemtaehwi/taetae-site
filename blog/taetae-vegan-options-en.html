Vegan and Vegetarian Options at Taetae (태태), Oksu-dong, Seoul

Written by the restaurant on 24 Nov 2026. Customer quotes are translated from Naver Place reviews.

Quick answer

At Taetae 태태, a Korean-Chinese restaurant in Oksu-dong, Seongdong-gu, Seoul, two dishes are vegan: Taetae Fish-Fragrant Eggplant (태태 어향가지, ₩23,000) and Taetae Jjajang (태태 짜장, ₩10,000). We do not state the ingredients of other dishes as vegetarian, so please ask the staff. This post is written by the restaurant; prices are as of November 2026.

Vegan dishes

  • Taetae Fish-Fragrant Eggplant (태태 어향가지), ₩23,000. Vegan. Our menu says to try it even if you do not like eggplant.
  • Taetae Jjajang (태태 짜장), ₩10,000. Vegan jjajangmyeon with a nutty stir-fried black bean sauce.

Other dishes

We have not marked any other dish as vegan or vegetarian. Many dishes contain meat, seafood or egg, such as the poached egg and pork belly gan-jjajang or the jjamppong soups. If you avoid specific ingredients, please tell the staff when you order, and ask about the sauce and broth of any dish you are considering.

FAQ

Does Taetae have vegan dishes?
Yes. Taetae Fish-Fragrant Eggplant (태태 어향가지, ₩23,000) and Taetae Jjajang (태태 짜장, ₩10,000) are vegan.
Are the other dishes vegetarian?
We have not stated that other dishes are vegetarian or vegan. Please ask the staff before ordering.
Can I order the vegan jjajangmyeon alone?
Yes, Taetae Jjajang is ₩10,000 as of November 2026. You can add rice (₩1,000) after confirming with staff.

What to Order at Taetae (태태), Oksu-dong, Seoul: Signature Dishes and Prices

Written by the restaurant on 24 Nov 2026. Customer quotes are translated from Naver Place reviews.

Quick answer

At Taetae 태태, a Korean-Chinese restaurant in Oksu-dong, Seongdong-gu, Seoul, the dishes most often recommended are Black Vinegar Sweet and Sour Pork (흑초 찹쌀 탕수육, ₩33,000), Gan-jjajang with Poached Egg and Pork Belly (수란 오겹 간짜장, ₩18,000) and Crab Jjamppong (태태 꽃게짬뽕, ₩17,000). Prices below are as of November 2026. This guide is written by the restaurant itself.

Signature dishes

  • Black Vinegar Sweet and Sour Pork (흑초 찹쌀 탕수육), ₩33,000. Our signature dish: a chewy, crisp glutinous-rice batter with black vinegar sauce.
  • Gan-jjajang with Poached Egg and Pork Belly (수란 오겹 간짜장), ₩18,000. Juicy, chewy pork belly with a poached egg on top.
  • Crab Jjamppong (태태 꽃게짬뽕), ₩17,000. A whole domestic crab from Boryeong, Chungcheongnam-do, in a spicy noodle soup.
  • Taetae Jjajang (태태 짜장), ₩10,000. Jjajangmyeon with a nutty stir-fried black bean sauce. It is vegan.

FAQ

What is the signature dish at Taetae?
Black Vinegar Sweet and Sour Pork (흑초 찹쌀 탕수육, ₩33,000), a chewy and crisp glutinous-rice fried pork with black vinegar sauce. The Gan-jjajang with Poached Egg and Pork Belly (₩18,000) is also widely recommended.
How much is Taetae jjajangmyeon?
Taetae Jjajang (태태 짜장) is ₩10,000 and the Gan-jjajang with Poached Egg and Pork Belly is ₩18,000 (as of November 2026).
Does Taetae have set menus?
Yes. There are sets for 1 (₩23,900), 2 (₩55,000) and 3 (₩89,000), and a Signature 3-Dish Set (₩97,000). Ask the staff for what each set includes.

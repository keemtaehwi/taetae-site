How to Book Taetae (태태) and Get There from Oksu Station Exit 7

Written by the restaurant on 24 Nov 2026. Customer quotes are translated from Naver Place reviews.

Quick answer

Taetae 태태 is a Korean-Chinese restaurant in Oksu-dong, Seongdong-gu, Seoul. You can reserve on Naver or Catchtable, or call +82 10-2503-6622. It is 340 m, about 5 minutes on foot, from Oksu Station Exit 7. This guide is written by the restaurant (prices and hours as of November 2026).

Groups and private hire (as of Nov 2026): Taetae takes group bookings for 20 to 30 people and private hire of the restaurant. Call +82 10-2503-6622 for private hire details.

How to book

  • Naver booking: https://m.place.naver.com/restaurant/1887736448/booking
  • Catchtable: https://app.catchtable.co.kr/ct/shop/taetae
  • Phone: +82 10-2503-6622

Walk-ins are welcome when tables are available.

Getting there from Oksu Station Exit 7

Oksu Station is on Line 3 and the Gyeongui-Jungang Line. From Exit 7 it is 340 m, about 5 minutes on foot. Taetae is on the 2nd floor of the building across the street from Raemian Oksu Riverzen, and you can spot the red 太太 sign. The last stretch is a slight uphill.

Address: 2F, 29 Hallimmal 3-gil, Seongdong-gu, Seoul (서울특별시 성동구 한림말3길 29 2층).

Parking

The building has no parking lot. The nearest option is the Oksu Riverzen shopping center parking lot. The cheapest is the public parking lot in front of Mega Coffee Oksu.

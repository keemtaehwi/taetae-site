TAETAE

Chinese Restaurant Near Hannam-dong and Itaewon (Yongsan-gu) for Group Dinners: Taetae, One Stop from Hannam Station

Taetae (태태 TAETAE), a Korean-Chinese restaurant in Oksu-dong, Seongdong-gu, Seoul, is one stop from Hannam Station on the Gyeongui-Jungang Line, so it works for Hannam-dong residents and expats. It has a table for 8 to 10 people, group bookings for 20 to 30 people, and private hire. Call +82 10-2503-6622 for group details.

Written by the Taetae team · Updated 2026-11-24 · Prices as of Nov 2026

How far is Taetae from Hannam-dong?

Take the Gyeongui-Jungang Line from Hannam Station and get off at Oksu Station, which is one stop away.

  • Oksu Station is served by Line 3 and the Gyeongui-Jungang Line.
  • On the Gyeongui-Jungang Line, Hannam and Eungbong are each one stop from Oksu.
  • On Line 3, Geumho and Apgujeong are each one stop from Oksu.
  • From Oksu Station Exit 7 it is 340m, about 5 minutes on foot.

The restaurant is on the 2nd floor of a building across the street from Raemian Oksu Riverzen, marked by a red 太太 sign. The last stretch to the door is a gentle uphill.

Can Taetae seat a large group?

Yes: there is a table for 8-10 people, group bookings of 20 to 30 people are available, and private hire can be arranged.

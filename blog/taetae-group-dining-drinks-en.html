Group Dining and Drinks at Taetae (태태), Oksu-dong, Seoul

Written by the restaurant on 24 Nov 2026. Customer quotes are translated from Naver Place reviews.

Quick answer

Taetae 태태, a Korean-Chinese restaurant in Oksu-dong, Seongdong-gu, Seoul, has several 4-seat tables, a 5-seat table and a group table for 8 to 10 people, plus high chairs. We serve highballs, natural wine, Korean traditional liquor and baijiu (kaoliang). This post is written by the restaurant; prices as of November 2026.

Groups and private hire (as of Nov 2026): Taetae takes group bookings for 20 to 30 people and private hire of the restaurant. Call +82 10-2503-6622 for private hire details.

Seating

  • Several 4-seat tables
  • 5-seat tables
  • A group table for 8 to 10 people
  • High chairs for small children
  • Reservations, takeout, delivery and wireless internet are available

FAQ

Can Taetae seat large groups?
Yes. There is a group table for 8 to 10 people, plus 4-seat and 5-seat tables. Please reserve in advance.
Does Taetae have high chairs?
Yes, high chairs are available.
What alcohol does Taetae serve?
Highballs (₩13,000), natural wine, Korean traditional liquor and baijiu (kaoliang). Ask staff for the current selection.

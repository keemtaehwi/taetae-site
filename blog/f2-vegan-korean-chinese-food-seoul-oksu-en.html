Vegan Korean-Chinese Food in Seoul: What Can a Vegan Eat at Taetae (태태 TAETAE)?

Taetae (태태 TAETAE), a Korean-Chinese restaurant in Oksu-dong, Seongdong-gu, Seoul, has two vegan dishes: Taetae Jjajang (태태 짜장, ₩10,000) and Fish-Fragrant Eggplant (태태 어향가지, ₩23,000). Other dishes are not marked vegan, so please ask the staff before ordering. It is about 5 minutes on foot (340m) from Exit 7 of Oksu Station.

Written by the Taetae team · Updated 2026-11-24 · Prices as of Nov 2026

Which dishes at Taetae are vegan?

Two dishes are vegan: Taetae Jjajang (태태 짜장) and Fish-Fragrant Eggplant (태태 어향가지).

DishPriceNotes
Taetae Jjajang (태태 짜장)₩10,000Vegan jjajangmyeon with a savory stir-fried black bean sauce
Fish-Fragrant Eggplant (태태 어향가지)₩23,000Vegan; the menu says to try it even if you dislike eggplant

A vegan pair for two people: one Taetae Jjajang each plus one Fish-Fragrant Eggplant to share.

Are the other dishes vegan or vegetarian?

No other dish is marked vegan on the menu, so ask the staff about ingredients before you order anything else.

  • Only the two dishes above are marked vegan.
  • Dishes such as Black Vinegar Sweet and Sour Pork (흑초 찹쌀 탕수육, ₩33,000) and Gan-jjajang with poached egg and pork belly (수란 오겹 간짜장, ₩18,000) are meat dishes.
  • Sets (1-person ₩23,900, 2-person ₩55,000, 3-person ₩89,000, signature 3-dish ₩97,000) are not marked vegan.

How do I get to Taetae and book a table?

Take Line 3 or the Gyeongui-Jungang Line to Oksu Station, leave by Exit 7, and walk 340m (about 5 minutes); booking is possible on Catchtable or Naver.

What are the opening hours and last orders?

Taetae is open every day, with lunch 11:00-15:00 (last order 14:30) and dinner 17:00-22:00 (last order 20:30).

  • Break time: 15:00-17:00.
  • Dinner last order is 20:30, so arrive early for the full menu.
  • No building parking; nearby lots are the Oksu Riverzen shopping-arcade lot (closest) and the public lot in front of Mega Coffee Oksu (cheapest).
Not a good fit if: Not a good fit if you need a fully vegan menu (only 2 dishes are vegan), if you arrive between 15:00 and 17:00 (break time), if you want dinner after the 20:30 last order, or if you plan to drive (no parking in the building).
